# Tournament Support

  - Technical troubleshooting guides
  - Match-related information
- Check the [Tournament Rules](/tournament-info/rules/)
  - Tournament regulations

  - Match format information

  - Qualifier and match guidelines

### Common Issues
- Map loading problems → [Troubleshooting Guide](/tournament-info/faq/#troubleshooting)
- Match scheduling → [Tournament Format](/tournament-info/format/)
- Technical difficulties → [FAQ](/tournament-info/faq/)

## Getting Help

If your question isn't answered in the documentation, you have several support options:

### Discord Support System (Recommended)

#### Support Tickets
- Create a ticket in #open-ticket channel
- Provide detailed information about your issue
- Wait for staff response
- Keep all communication in your ticket

#### General Help Channel
- Use #questions-suggestions for general inquiries
- Check pinned messages for common solutions
- Be patient while waiting for responses

### Direct Staff Contact

For department-specific issues:
- Check the [Staff List](/tournament-info/staff/) to find the appropriate department head
- Contact them directly with your concerns
- For general inquiries, contact RaiRei (Internal Affairs)

When contacting staff directly:
- Ensure your issue matches their department
- Include relevant details in your initial message
- Be patient waiting for a response

### Technical Support

#### Map Issues
- Follow the [Troubleshooting Guide](/tournament-info/faq/#troubleshooting)
- Contact a referee if problems persist
- Document any error messages

#### Client Problems
- Ensure osu! is updated
- Verify game files
- Contact match referee if issues occur during a match

### Reporting Issues

#### Player/Team Reports
- Gather evidence (screenshots, timestamps, chat logs)
- Use the ticket system or contact relevant staff
- Provide clear description of incidents

#### Staff Reports
- Gather all available evidence
- Contact RaiRei directly through Discord
- Do not use ticket system or other staff members
- All reports handled confidentially

## Best Practices

### When Seeking Help
- Be clear and concise
- Include relevant details (match times, maps, players)
- Provide screenshots when applicable
- Use appropriate Discord channels
- Avoid spamming multiple channels
- Tag only relevant staff members

### Communication
- Monitor your support channel
- Respond promptly to staff questions
- Mark tickets as resolved when fixed
- Follow up politely if needed
- Maintain professional conduct

## Emergency Contact

For urgent match-day issues:
1. Contact your assigned referee
2. If unavailable, ping @Referee in #general
3. For serious issues, contact:
