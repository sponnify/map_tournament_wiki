## Tournament Schedule

## Scheduling Information

### Deadlines
{: .warning }
> - Match reschedule requests must be submitted before **Thursday 23:59 UTC** of the match week
> - Teams should confirm or reschedule their matches as early as possible to ensure referee availability

---

## Tournament Breaks

### Lunar New Year Break
{: .note }
The tournament will have a week-long break from **Monday, January 27th** to **Sunday, February 2nd, 2025** to accommodate Lunar New Year celebrations.

During this period:
- Staff may be less active
- Response times may be slower
- No matches will be scheduled

## Important Reminders

- All times are in **UTC**
- Match schedules must be confirmed by both teams
- Early scheduling is strongly encouraged
- Referees must be available for all official matches
