# Important Dates

## Tournament Schedule

### Registration
- Registration Opens: Friday, January 10th, 2025
- Registration Closes: Sunday, February 2nd, 2025

### Match Schedule
All tournament matches take place between Friday 23:59 UTC and Monday 23:59 UTC on their designated weekends.

### Tournament Stages
- Qualifiers: Friday, February 7th - Monday, February 10th, 2025
- Round of 32: Friday, February 14th - Monday, February 17th, 2025
- Round of 16: Friday, February 21st - Monday, February 24th, 2025
- Quarter Finals: Friday, February 28th - Monday, March 3rd, 2025
- Semi Finals: Friday, March 7th - Monday, March 10th, 2025
- Finals: Friday, March 14th - Monday, March 17th, 2025
- Grand Finals: Friday, March 21st - Monday, March 24th, 2025

### Scheduling Deadlines
- Match reschedule requests must be submitted before Thursday 23:59 UTC of the match week
- Teams should confirm or reschedule their matches as early as possible

## Tournament Breaks
- Lunar New Year Break: Monday, January 27th - Sunday, February 2nd, 2025

Please note that there will be a week-long tournament break from Monday, January 27th to Sunday, February 2nd, 2025, to accommodate Lunar New Year celebrations. Staff may be inactive, and/or less likely to respond during this time.
