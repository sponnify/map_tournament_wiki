## Mapper's Favourite Bounty System

{: .important }
Each round of the tournament features an opportunity to win additional prizes through the "**Mapper's Favourite**" bounty system.

### Overview
- A *special map* is selected by the mapping board each round as the "Mapper's Favourite"
- Players can earn extra rewards by beating score thresholds on these maps
- **Two winners** per round will each receive **1 month** of osu!supporter
- Total bounty prize pool: **14 months** of osu!supporter across the tournament

### How to Participate

#### 1. Map Selection & Announcement
- The Mapper's Favourite map will be shown in the mappool showcase video, and announced in the round's announcement post
- Each announcement includes the map and its required score threshold
- Players must meet or *exceed* this threshold to qualify for the raffle

#### 2. Attempt Rules
{: .note }
- Attempts must occur during **official tournament matches**
- Teams can make their attempt after the match winner is decided, but within the *same match lobby*
- Each team gets **exactly one attempt** per bounty map
- Maximum **4 players** per team may participate in a bounty attempt
- All participating players must attempt the map *simultaneously*
- For teams playing the same bounty map multiple times (e.g., in losers' bracket), only the *first attempt* counts

#### 3. Prize Distribution
- Raffles conducted via livestream during the following round's mappool showcase
- **Two winners** randomly selected from all qualifying attempts
- Players may only win **one bounty per round**
- Winning a bounty does *not* affect eligibility for tournament placement prizes
