# Screening

{: .warning }
Screening results will be announced on **February 3rd, 2025 at 23:59 UTC**

---

## Screening Process

### Overview
{: .note }
The MAP Tournament uses **manual screening** performed by our screening team on all registrants. Exact screening criteria will remain **hidden until screening concludes** to prevent manipulation.

### Timeline
1. Registration closes
2. Manual screening begins
3. Results announced via Discord
4. Appeal window opens

---

## Screening Criteria

### Primary Evaluation Areas

#### 1. Rank Verification
- Must be between **75,000** and **999,999**
- Checked for rank manipulation patterns
- No rank buffer provided

#### 2. Account Activity

| Factor | What We Check |
|:--------|:---------------|
| Account Age | Account creation date and activity history |
| Play History | Playtime and count distribution |
| Activity Patterns | Recent activity and inactivity periods |
| Restriction History | Past restrictions and their resolution |
| ETX Patterns | Unusual or suspicious patterns |

#### 3. Tournament History
- Previous tournament participation
- Past behavioral records
- Performance consistency
- Community conduct

#### 4. Performance Analysis
- ETX ratings evaluation
- Top play patterns
- Skill consistency checks
- Tournament performance history

{: .important }
Our screening team may use reasonable discretion to screen players who raise concerns even if they don't meet specific criteria.

---

## Eligibility Requirements

### Basic Requirements
- Rank between 75,000-999,999
- Clean restriction history
- Account in good standing
- Tournament rule compliance
- Good community conduct

{: .warning }
Failing to maintain these requirements until registration closes will result in automatic screening.

---

## Appeal Process

### How to Appeal
1. Open a ticket in **#open-ticket**
2. Include required information:
   - osu! username
   - Known screening reason
   - Appeal justification
   - Supporting evidence

### Appeal Guidelines
{: .note }
> - All appeals are reviewed individually
> - Decisions are final
> - Harassment results in immediate denial
> - Multiple appeals are not accepted

### Required Information

| Component | Description |
|:-----------|:-------------|
| Username | Your full osu! username |
| Reason | Why you were screened (if known) |
| Justification | Why the decision should be reconsidered |
| Evidence | Any relevant supporting information |

{: .warning }
Appeals lacking any required information will be automatically denied.
