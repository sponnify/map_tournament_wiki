# Screening

## Screening Criteria
* The MAP Tournament uses **manual screening** performed by our screening team on all registrants.
* Exact screening criteria will remain **hidden until screening concludes** to prevent criteria manipulation.
* Players may be screened for:
   * **Rank concerns**
      * Being outside rank range (75k-999k)
      * Rank manipulation patterns
   * **Suspicious account activity**
      * Young account age
      * Low playtime/playcount
      * Extended account inactivity
      * Recently revoked restrictions
      * Suspicious ETX patterns
   * **Tournament history**
      * Recent tournament bans
      * History of toxic behavior
      * Notable performance concerns
      * Poor previous conduct
   * **Performance concerns**
      * Unusually high ETX ratings
      * Suspicious top plays (standard/tournament)
      * Inconsistent skill demonstration
* Our screening team may use **reasonable discretion** to screen players who don't meet specific criteria but raise other concerns.

## Screening Format
* All players will be manually screened by our screening team after registration closes
* Screening results will be announced on February 3rd, 2025 at 23:59 UTC
* Players will be notified of screening results through Discord
* Players who pass screening will be able to participate in the tournament
* Players who don't pass screening may appeal through our appeal process

## Eligibility 
To be eligible for the tournament, players must:
* Have a rank between 75,000 and 999,999 when registrations close
* Have not been restricted in the recent past
* Have an account in good standing
* Follow all tournament rules and guidelines
* Maintain good conduct in the community

## Appeals
* Screened players can appeal by opening a ticket in the **#open-ticket** channel in our Discord server
* Appeals should include:
   * Your osu! username
   * Reason for screening (if known)
   * Why you believe the screening decision should be reconsidered
   * Any relevant evidence supporting your appeal
* **Appeal decisions are final** and harassment during appeals will result in immediate denial
