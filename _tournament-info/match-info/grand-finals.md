# Grand Finals Scheduling

{: .important }
The following process applies to the **top 6 teams** determined after Semifinals. All scheduling must be coordinated through our Match Coordinator & Internal Affairs Manager, [RaiRei](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/24156254).

---

## Initial Requirements

| Aspect | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **When** | *Immediately* after final Semifinals match |
| **Who** | All six remaining Finals teams |
| **Where** | Designated threads in #grand-finals-week |

{: .note }
Each captain must plan schedules with **all five** potential opponents.

---

## Scheduling Process

### Captain Requirements
Team captains must:
- Begin discussions *immediately* after top 6 confirmation
- Contact **each** potential opponent
- Propose and discuss viable match times
- Consider *all reasonable* time proposals
- Work towards mutually beneficial agreements

{: .warning }
All potential matchup schedules must be **finalized** by the end of Finals week.

---

## Schedule Management

### Schedule Finalization
Once Grand Finalists are determined:
- Teams must confirm previously agreed schedule with **RaiRei**
- Schedule changes require:
  - Approval from *both* teams
  - Staff confirmation
- Default to Finals week schedule if teams cannot agree on changes

### Delay Requests
Teams may request a **one-week delay** when submitting final schedule.

Requirements:
- Valid justification provided
- Agreement from *both* teams
- Staff approval

---

## Fair Play Policies

### Unfair Practices
{: .warning }
The following are considered unfair scheduling practices:
- Misrepresenting team availability
- Pressuring teams into disadvantageous times
- Using forfeit threats as leverage
- Deliberately exploiting known team weaknesses
- Making unreasonable demands
- Showing unwillingness to compromise

### Penalties
Teams found scheduling unfairly may face:
- Forfeiture of Grand Finals spot
- Replacement by previous opponent

{: .important }
Penalty decisions require:
- Clear evidence of unfair practices
- **Full** staff team review
- *Unanimous* staff agreement
