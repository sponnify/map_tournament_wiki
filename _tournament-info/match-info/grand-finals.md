# Grand Finals Scheduling

The following process applies to the top 6 teams determined after the conclusion of Semifinals. All scheduling must be coordinated through our Match Coordinator & Internal Affairs Manager, [RaiRei](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/24156254).

### Initial Requirements
* **When**: Immediately after the final Semifinals match concludes
* **Who**: All six remaining teams in Finals
* **Where**: All discussions must take place in designated threads in #grand-finals-week
* Each captain must plan schedules with all five potential opponents

### Scheduling Process
* Team captains must:
  * Begin discussions immediately after top 6 is confirmed
  * Contact each potential opponent
  * Propose and discuss viable match times
  * Consider all reasonable time proposals
  * Work towards mutually beneficial agreements
* All potential matchup schedules must be finalized by the end of Finals week

### Schedule Finalization 
* Once Grand Finalists are determined:
  * Teams must confirm their previously agreed schedule with RaiRei
  * Any schedule changes require approval from both teams and staff
  * If teams cannot agree on a new time, the previously agreed schedule from Finals week will be enforced

### Delay Requests
* Teams may request a one-week delay when submitting their final schedule
* Delay requests must:
  * Include valid justification
  * Be agreed upon by both teams
  * Be approved by staff

### Fair Scheduling Policy
Teams must schedule in good faith. Examples of unfair practices include:
* Misrepresenting team availability
* Pressuring teams into disadvantageous times
* Using forfeit threats as leverage
* Deliberately scheduling to exploit known team weaknesses
* Making unreasonable demands
* Showing unwillingness to compromise

### Penalties
* Teams found scheduling unfairly may:
  * Forfeit their Grand Finals spot
  * Be replaced by their previous opponent
* Such decisions require:
  * Clear evidence of unfair practices
  * Full staff team review
  * Unanimous staff agreement
