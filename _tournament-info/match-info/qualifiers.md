# Qualifier Stage

The qualifier stage determines team seeding for the main tournament bracket. Each team must register for a qualifier timeslot through the tournament Discord server. A referee will be supplied for each registered qualifier match.

---

## Schedule Information

{: .warning }
Qualifier matches will **only** be held between **Friday, February 7th, 2025, 00:00 UTC** and **Sunday, February 9th, 2025, 23:59 UTC**. This time window is *strictly enforced*—no exceptions will be made for matches outside these hours.

---

## Match Preparation

### Before the Match

| Time | Action |
|:-----|:--------|
| **15 minutes** before | Referee will contact team captain via Discord |
| **5 minutes** before | Referee will create the lobby and invite the captain |
| Until start | Captain is responsible for inviting their team members |

### Captain Substitution
If the team captain cannot attend:
- Notify the referee *in advance*
- Designate a temporary captain
- Provide the temporary captain's osu! username

### Late Penalties
Teams must have **4 players** ready to play at their scheduled time. Late penalties vary based on lobby type:

#### Shared Lobbies
If a team arrives late to a lobby that has already started:
- They may still participate if their **4th player** arrives before the **NoMod** section ends
- The team will play alongside other teams and complete any missed maps after the regular lobby ends
- If unable to field **4 players** before NoMod section ends, team must reschedule

#### Solo Team Lobbies
If a team is **15 minutes late**:
- Their qualifier lobby will be *canceled*
- Team must attempt to reschedule

{: .note }
**Rescheduling Notes**:
- Late rescheduling requests or requests for extra lobbies are *not guaranteed*
- Staff will attempt to accommodate but cannot guarantee availability
- Teams should arrive on time to their original slot to ensure participation

## Team Requirements

### Team Size and Composition
- Teams must have between **4-8 players** total
- A minimum of **4 players** must be available for each map
- Teams may rotate players between maps
- *All* players must be registered team members

### Match Participation
- **4 players** must actively participate in each map
- While not all team members need to be present for matches, the minimum of **4 players** is required

---

## Match Format

The qualifier match consists of one complete playthrough of the qualifier mappool, with an option for a second playthrough to improve scores.

### Mappool Order
Maps must be played in this sequence:
1. **NoMod**: NM1, NM2, NM3, NM4
2. **Hidden**: HD1, HD2
3. **HardRock**: HR1, HR2
4. **DoubleTime**: DT1, DT2

### Between Maps
- Teams have **120 seconds** to prepare for each map
- Referee will start the map within **10 seconds** after this preparation period
- All maps require **4 players** to participate

### Second Playthrough
After completing all maps, teams may:
- Take a **5-minute** break
- Select specific maps to improve their scores
- Use different players than their first playthrough
- Skip maps they don't wish to replay

The second playthrough must:
- Begin *immediately* after the break
- Follow the original mappool order
- Have maps selected for replay declared by the captain before starting

## Match Settings

{: .important }
All qualifier maps will be played with:
- **Head-to-Head** scoring
- **NoFail** mod enabled
- **ScoreV2** enabled
- **Team VS** mode

---

## Technical Issues

### Disconnections
- If a player disconnects during a map due to technical issues, they may replay that map after all qualifier maps are completed
- To prevent abuse of this system, *only one player* may use this replay rule for *one map per team*
- For any additional disconnections:
  - The disconnected player will receive a score of **0**
  - Exception: If suitable proof of score can be provided (e.g., local score screenshot with time/date)

{: .note }
This replay limitation exists to prevent teams from strategically using "disconnections" to improve scores by replaying maps multiple times.

---

## Scoring & Results

Results determine bracket stage seeding:
- Teams must complete **one full playthrough** with **4 players** per map
- For teams completing two playthroughs:
  - Most recent score for each map will be used
- Seeding will be calculated using the **Zipfian Distribution**
- Teams will be **disqualified** for:
  - Not scheduling a qualifier lobby
  - Not completing one full playthrough
  - Not having 4 players for each map
